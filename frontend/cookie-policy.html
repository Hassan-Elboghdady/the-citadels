Cookie Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently and to provide information to the site owners.

2. Cookies We Use

We use a minimal number of cookies, limited to what is necessary for the website to function and to respect your preferences.

Essential Cookies

Cookie Name Purpose Duration
cookie_consent Stores your cookie consent preference so we do not ask you again on every visit. 365 days

Essential cookies are necessary for the website to function. They cannot be disabled.

Analytics Cookies (Optional)

If you accept cookies through our consent banner, we may use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the website. These cookies collect anonymous, aggregated data only.

Cookie Name Purpose Duration
_ga Google Analytics: distinguishes unique users 2 years
_ga_* Google Analytics: maintains session state 2 years

Analytics cookies are only set if you click "Accept" on our cookie consent banner. If you click "Decline", no analytics cookies are set.

3. Third-Party Cookies

Our website does not embed third-party content (such as social media widgets or video players) that would set additional cookies. Links to external services such as Google Maps or WhatsApp open in a new tab and are governed by those services' own cookie policies.

4. How to Manage Cookies

You can manage cookies through:

  • Our cookie consent banner Click "Decline" to reject non-essential cookies. Your preference is saved and the banner will not appear again.
  • Your browser settings Most browsers allow you to block or delete cookies. Refer to your browser's help documentation for instructions. Note that blocking essential cookies may affect website functionality.

To reset your cookie preference on our website, clear the cookie_consent item from your browser's local storage.

5. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy if we change the cookies we use. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
