Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

The Citadels ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard information when you use our website.

The Citadels is a company registered in Egypt with its head office at 10 Sicily Street, off Makram Ebeid Street, Nasr City, Cairo 11765.

2. What Data We Collect

We collect only the data necessary to respond to your inquiries and provide our services:

  • Full name to address you in our response
  • Phone number to contact you about your inquiry
  • Email address to send you a written response
  • Inquiry type to direct your inquiry to the right team member
  • Message content to understand your requirements
  • Preferred contact method to reach you through your preferred channel

We do not collect data beyond what is submitted through our contact form. We do not use hidden tracking pixels, fingerprinting, or behavioral tracking.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use the data you provide exclusively to:

  • Respond to your inquiry or order
  • Provide information about our furniture products and services
  • Follow up on project discussions you have initiated

We do not use your data for marketing purposes unless you explicitly opt in to receive updates.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your personal data based on your explicit consent, which you provide by checking the consent checkbox on our contact form. You may withdraw your consent at any time by contacting us.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with any third parties, except:

  • When required by Egyptian law or a court order
  • To protect the rights or safety of The Citadels, our clients, or the public

6. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely and access is limited to authorized personnel who need it to respond to your inquiry. We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, loss, or destruction.

We retain your inquiry data for a maximum of 24 months after your last interaction with us, after which it is deleted.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction request correction of inaccurate data
  • Deletion request deletion of your personal data
  • Withdrawal of consent withdraw your consent to data processing at any time

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

8. Cookies

Our website uses cookies. For detailed information, please see our Cookie Policy.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
